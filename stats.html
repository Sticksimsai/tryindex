Points

Every trade on the curve earns points. Points set your share of the weekly basket drop and of the platform's vested slice of every launch.

  • Trade — 1 point per $1 of curve volume
  • Hold through graduation — 2× on tokens you held from curve to Raydium
  • Launch — graduated launches earn their creator a standing boost
